The deadlock chain was: `DocSynchronizer` holds `docLock` → calls `_updateDocument(doc)` → `Repo.UpdateDocument` → `handle.NotifyRemoteChange(document)` → tries to acquire same `_documentLock` = DEADLOCK. Fix: Made `NotifyRemoteChange` async, moved `_updateDocument(doc)` OUTSIDE the docLock scope in `ReceiveSyncMessage`, changed delegate chain to `Func<AutomergeDocument, Task>` throughout DocSynchronizer → CollectionSynchronizer → Repo.
